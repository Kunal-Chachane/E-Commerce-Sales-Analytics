# 📊 Global E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence dashboard** designed to analyze global e-commerce sales performance and uncover actionable business insights.

The dashboard transforms transactional data into interactive visualizations covering **sales, profit, orders, products, customers, regions, countries, and payment methods**.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project provides stakeholders with a centralized view of e-commerce performance and helps identify trends, top-performing markets, profitable products, and customer purchasing patterns.

The dashboard is organized into three analytical sections:

- 🏠 **Overview** — Overall business performance and KPIs
- 🌍 **Geography** — Country and regional sales analysis
- 📦 **Products** — Product and category performance

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- 📊 **Power BI**
- 📐 **DAX**
- 🧹 **Power Query**
- 🗄️ **SQL**
- 📑 **Excel / CSV**

## 📈 Key KPIs

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| 💰 Total Sales | Overall revenue generated |
| 📈 Total Profit | Total profit generated |
| 🛒 Total Orders | Number of customer orders |
| 📦 Total Quantity | Total products sold |
| 💵 Average Order Value | Average revenue per order |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🏠 Overview

Provides a high-level snapshot of overall business performance.

- KPI Cards for Sales, Profit, Orders & Quantity
- Top Countries by Sales
- Sales by Region
- Payment Method Analysis
- Interactive Filters & Slicers
- Dynamic Page Navigation

### 🌍 Geography

Provides detailed geographical performance analysis.

- Regional Sales Analysis
- Country-wise Sales Performance
- Geographic Comparisons
- Identification of High-Performing Markets
- Interactive Country & Region Filtering

### 📦 Products

Analyzes product-level and category-level performance.

- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Category-wise Sales
- Product Performance Comparison
- Quantity Sold Analysis
- Revenue Contribution by Category

---

## 🧠 Business Insights

The dashboard enables businesses to:

- Identify the **highest-performing countries** by sales.
- Compare **regional sales and profitability**.
- Determine which **product categories contribute the most revenue**.
- Identify **top-performing products**.
- Analyze **customer payment preferences**.
- Monitor important business KPIs from a single dashboard.
- Use interactive filtering for faster and more effective decision-making.
